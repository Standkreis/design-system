# Brand assets and reuse

The implementation and documentation are offered under the MIT licence, with upstream notices preserved. Titillium Web files retain their SIL Open Font License in `packages/ui/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.

Reuse and adaptation of the components and patterns are welcome. Standkreis projects guide the library's priorities.

The Standkreis name and logo identify Standkreis and its applications. The code licence does not grant permission to imply Standkreis affiliation or endorsement. For unrelated products, use your own identity. Contact the organisation before using Standkreis as your product identity.

The Gathered circle with a central dot is the selected Standkreis mark. Its three arcs are equal and equally spaced, with one opening directly right. The centre dot is part of the identity. Use the canonical `standkreis-mark.svg` asset or the shared Brand/BrandMark components.

The reference app includes photographs under the Unsplash License, separately from the installable UI package. See [imagery provenance](docs/IMAGERY.md) for sources and usage terms.
